🖱️ Virtual Mouse

How to use:
1. Click "Start Virtual Mouse".
2. Allow webcam access.
3. Move your index finger.
4. The red cursor follows your finger.
5. Bring your thumb and index finger together to click.

⌨️ Virtual Keyboard

How to use:
1. Click "Start Virtual Keyboard".
2. Allow webcam access.
3. Point your index finger at a key.
4. Bring your thumb and index finger together.
5. The selected key will be typed below.
